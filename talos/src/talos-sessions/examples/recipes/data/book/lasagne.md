# Lasagne

Serves 4.

## Ingredients

- 500 g beef mince
- 1 onion, chopped
- 2 garlic cloves
- 400 g chopped tomatoes
- 12 lasagne sheets
- 500 ml milk
- 40 g butter
- 40 g plain flour
- 100 g cheddar, grated

## Method

1. Brown the mince with the onion and garlic, add the tomatoes, simmer 20 minutes.
2. Melt the butter, stir in the flour, whisk in the milk until thick.
3. Layer sauce, sheets and white sauce; finish with cheese.
4. Bake at 190°C for 40 minutes.

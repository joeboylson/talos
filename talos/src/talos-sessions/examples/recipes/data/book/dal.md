# Red lentil dal

Serves 2.

## Ingredients

- 150 g red lentils
- 1 onion, sliced
- 1 tsp cumin seeds
- 1 tsp turmeric
- 400 ml coconut milk
- 1 lime

## Method

1. Fry the onion and cumin until soft.
2. Add lentils, turmeric, coconut milk and 200 ml water; simmer 20 minutes.
3. Finish with lime juice.
